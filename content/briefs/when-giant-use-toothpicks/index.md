# When Giants Use Toothpicks

Fireblocks is a digital asset custody platform, protecting trillions of dollars. During a security review at work I found how they protect their 4096-bit RSA 'disaster recovery' keys for customers with 2 rounds of MD5.

Following our responsible disclosure, Fireblocks has patched the issue.

The full technical breakdown and disclosure timeline is on our blog: [When Giants Use Toothpicks](https://blog.senthorus.ch/posts/when_giant_use_toothpicks/)
